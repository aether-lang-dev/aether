- **`${}` segments of an interpolated string now evaluate left to right, in source order, on every C compiler and optimisation level.** They were lowered to the varargs of one `printf` / `_aether_interp` call, and C leaves argument evaluation order unspecified, so gcc ran `"${bump(c)} ${bump(c)} ${bump(c)}"` right to left and printed `3 2 1`. Once any segment has a side effect, every segment is now evaluated into a temp in source order ahead of the call; an all-pure interpolation is unchanged. A nested interpolation used as a segment is also freed after the enclosing call instead of leaking. (#2195)
