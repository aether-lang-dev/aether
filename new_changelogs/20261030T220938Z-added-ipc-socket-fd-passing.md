- `std.ipc` gained a bidirectional socket channel and file descriptor passing on Linux, FreeBSD, and macOS. `os.run_socket` spawns children with a connected `AF_UNIX` stream socket pair, and `os.socketpair` creates in-process socket pairs. `std.ipc` gained `parent_channel_kind()`, binary-safe `read()`, `send_fd()`, and `recv_fd()`, while preserving `parent_channel()`, `write()`, and `write_close()` for backward-compatible pipe channels. Descriptor passing enforces strict safety rules: exactly one descriptor per message with leak prevention, `FD_CLOEXEC` on receipt, `SIGPIPE` resistance, short write retries, empty payload rejection, and descriptive error messages.
