- **contrib.vulkan, contrib.d3d12, contrib.metal: BC6H, signed formats, ETC2
  and ASTC, and cubes and arrays in any format.** HDR skies and lightmaps,
  two-channel normal maps, and assets compressed for Apple and mobile GPUs
  can now be sampled through the shared shape.
  - **Formats:** `FORMAT_BC6H_UFLOAT` and `_SFLOAT`; `FORMAT_R8_SNORM`,
    `FORMAT_R8G8_SNORM`, `FORMAT_BC4_SNORM` and `FORMAT_BC5_SNORM`; and the
    ETC2 RGB and RGBA and ASTC 4x4 formats, where the GPU samples them.
    Direct3D 12 has no ETC2 or ASTC.
  - **Cubes and arrays:** `texture_create_cube_format` and
    `texture_create_array_format` take any of these formats.
  - **Testing:**
    - signed texels read -1 to 1, and a signed mip chain averages as signed;
    - hand-encoded BC6H, signed BC4, ETC2 and ASTC void-extent blocks decode
      to the colours computed for them;
    - an sRGB array reads linear light;
    - a BC1 cube's six faces read back.
    These run on every backend that has the format (#2402).
