- **Aether runs on the ESP32, under emulation.** The generated C now builds
  as an ESP-IDF component for the ESP32 (Xtensa LX6): under `ESP_PLATFORM` it
  supplies the `app_main()` entry point ESP-IDF calls, and leaves out the
  terminal raw-mode helpers (ESP-IDF's termios is compiled out by default).
  The scheduler's table sizes (`MAX_CORES`, `MAX_ACTORS_PER_CORE`,
  `SCHEDULER_DRAIN_BATCH`, `QUEUE_SIZE`) can be overridden with `-D`: at the
  defaults its static tables need about 10 MB, and an ESP32 has about 320 KB
  of DRAM. `make ci-esp32` (and a new CI job) builds a hello-world and three
  actor programs into firmware in the official `espressif/idf` image and boots
  them in Espressif's QEMU, checking their output. See `docs/getting-started.md`.
