- **A send to a client that stopped reading is bounded by the send timeout on NetBSD.** NetBSD's `send(2)` ignores `MSG_DONTWAIT` on a TCP socket, so `std.http`'s response writes and `tcp.write_all` blocked in the kernel instead of waiting in `poll` under the send timeout (#3257): a stalled client got the whole response once it read again. On NetBSD the socket is made non-blocking for the write.
