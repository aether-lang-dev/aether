- **`math.random_int` and `math.random_float` are safe to call from several threads.** The splitmix64 state that replaced `rand()` was a plain global, so two actors drawing at once could lose a step and get the same number, and two first draws could both seed from the clock. The step is an atomic add and the first draw seeds once; a single thread sees the same sequence after `random_seed` as before.
