- **`grant_runtime("python3")`: what a foreign interpreter needs to start, with no platform paths in the host.** In a grant list's builder it asks the interpreter itself (`python3 -I -c ...`, run unsandboxed when the list is built) for the directories on its `sys.path` and its libpython, and grants them for reading: `/usr/lib/python3.14/*` on Arch, `/usr/lib/python3.10/*` on Ubuntu, a Homebrew or pyenv prefix wherever it is. A table says only which interpreters can be asked (`python3`, `python`); another name, or an interpreter that cannot be asked, panics with the reason. It grants no exec and not the guest's script, which stay the host's to grant. Aether workers need no preset: they lock themselves down with `sandbox.enforce` after they start.
