- **The sandbox preload refuses a `dlopen` of libc by any name, not only `libc.so.6`.** Without a native grant it compared the name with `libc.so.6` and `libc.so`, so `ctypes.CDLL("/usr/lib/libc.so.6")`, the `/lib` twin of that path, a `..`, or a symlink with another name handed a guest libc, and `dlsym` on it the real `open` past the preload. It now matches libc and the dynamic loader (glibc, musl's `libc.musl-*`, `ld-linux*`, `ld-musl-*`, FreeBSD's `libc.so.7` and `ld-elf.so.1`) by the basename of the name, by the basename of where a path leads, and by the handle the loader returns, which catches a name found on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`. This is the preload layer; on Linux Landlock under `sandbox.spawn_socket` is the wall a raw `openat` meets.
