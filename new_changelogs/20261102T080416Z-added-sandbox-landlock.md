- **Landlock under the sandboxed spawns (Linux 5.13+, no root, no user namespace).** `sandbox.spawn_socket` and `spawn_sandboxed` turn the fs and exec grants into Landlock rules in the child before it execs, so an `openat` the preload never sees (a raw syscall instruction, ctypes reaching libc's own `syscall`) is refused by the kernel. Network rules (connect on a granted TCP port) are used from ABI 4 (Linux 6.7), and abstract Unix sockets and signals are scoped from ABI 6. The child can still exec its program: Landlock gets the program, its ELF interpreter (or a `#!` script's), the libraries beside that interpreter (read, not listed), `/etc/ld.so.cache`, the locale archive, `/dev/null`, `/dev/urandom` and the preload library. A grant Landlock cannot express is never widened: a suffix pattern (`*.txt`) is refused with a reason, a name prefix (`/usr/lib/python3*`) covers the entries that match when the child starts, and an exact directory gets no rule. On a kernel without Landlock, `spawn_socket` refuses unless the host passes the new `grant_without_landlock()`; `spawn_sandboxed` keeps running its child, with a warning on stderr.
